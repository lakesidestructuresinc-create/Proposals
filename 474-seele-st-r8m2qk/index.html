What we found on November 2

Fill piled on the upper bank and runoff from the clogged drainage path have cut the bank into near-vertical faces. Beside Building B, the bank has eroded under the footing of the concrete drainage structure.

Renderings

What the finished bank will look like

Two sections of C-loc seawall, tied into the concrete drainage structure so they form one continuous line. Phase 1 is the tall section beside Building B, running from the drainage structure up along the eroded bank toward the spiral staircase. Phase 2 runs along the lower bank on the lawn side, with a limestone block wall higher up where the top of the bank is cut down.

Wall alignments per Lakeside's November 2 markup on the drone photo: Phase 1 in blue, Phase 2 C-loc in amber, and the Phase 2 limestone wall dashed. Final layout is field-verified before construction.
