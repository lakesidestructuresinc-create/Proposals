    Sheet A.4 · Detail

    Your tandem jet ski cradle

    Two jet skis park nose-in, one behind the other. The ski at the shore end rides on a raised back-lift set of bunks, so both fit in the 6′ slip and lift out of the water together.

    • Lower bunksTwo HSS 6″ × 2″ × ¼″ steel bunks, 18′-0″ long and 2′-1″ apart, carry the lake-side ski.
    • Back-lift bunksTwo 9′-0″ bunks on welded plate risers raise the shore-side ski above the other.
    • Lift beamsTwo 4′-5″ lift beams with welded plate gussets hold the bunks together.
    • Lift cablesPlate lift lugs and four wire cables raise the whole cradle with both skis.
